# ADR 0019: Approved Investment Committee roles

Status: **Accepted — SETTLED by product owner**. Date: 2026-09-05.

## Context and history

Refines ADR-0007/0010 and settles the conceptual role portion of OD-08.

## Decision

Use Market Evidence Analyst, Event Context Analyst, Business Context Analyst, Challenger, Historical Evidence Analyst and Adjudicator. Required specialists can run concurrently on frozen evidence; Adjudicator follows required validated returns. Measured policies need not invoke every specialist.

## Alternatives

Majority voting and LLM numerical feature computation remain prohibited. The Challenger seeks contradictions, assumptions, alternatives and invalidation, not rubber-stamp consensus.

## Consequences and remaining decisions

Exact required subsets, escalation/failure deadlines and model vendors remain open. Outputs remain evidence/proposals, never order or risk authority.
