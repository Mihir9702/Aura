# ADR 0022: Offline League and multi-metric evidence scorecards

Status: **Accepted — SETTLED by product owner**. Date: 2026-09-05.

## Context and history

Extends ADR-0012/0013 and closes evaluation direction in OD-15 while preserving numeric qualification questions.

## Decision

League/scorecards are offline research governance, never direct order authority or raw reward for increasing risk. Adopt multi-metric returns/benchmark/drawdown/expectancy/cost/sample/uncertainty/regime/calibration scorecards with no-AI and Shadow comparisons. Evidence may inform owner-approved versioned qualification/allocation policy.

## Alternatives

Raw profit ranking and live score-to-weight feedback encourage unmeasured risk and selection bias. Automatic promotion from winning scores is rejected.

## Consequences and remaining decisions

Exact metric conventions, benchmarks, thresholds and qualification evidence remain open. Strategies owns eligibility, Portfolio Manager/Risk own capital decisions; evaluations cannot mutate them.
